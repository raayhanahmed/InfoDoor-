About Us

We are The Loop Troops,a team of five undergraduate students from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University, developing InfoDoor, a Student Management System. Our project focuses on efficient and user-friendly management of student and teacher records, including adding, updating, deleting, and viewing detailed information. Through this work, we have applied our skills in programming and system design while enhancing our teamwork and problem-solving abilities.
